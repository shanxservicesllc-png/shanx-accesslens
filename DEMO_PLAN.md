# AccessLens | Demo outline (2 minutes 30 seconds)

**Status:** Proposed owner-reviewed demonstration plan. Not a recorded video and not entrant-written Devpost submission text.

**00:00–00:20 | The problem.** Show the home screen and say who AccessLens is for: someone publishing a small HTML page who wants to spot common accessibility issues early. Point out the **no score** and **local** disclosures.

**00:20–00:55 | Flawed page.** Click **Load flawed example**, then **Inspect HTML**. Show 5 findings: 3 high (image alt, empty link, unlabeled input), 1 medium (duplicate id), 1 review (heading h1 → h3). Focus on a specific element excerpt.

**00:55–01:30 | Precise repairs.** Scroll a finding, inspect its reason, copy its example repair. Filter to medium findings; show the duplicate ID conflict. Emphasize the suggestions must be reviewed before applying to a real page.

**01:30–01:55 | Export.** Choose All findings, select **Export report .md**, and open/show the report. Confirm the original HTML was not modified.

**01:55–02:18 | Before and after.** Click **Load corrected example** and **Inspect HTML**. Show zero findings in the same five static checks, not a WCAG certificate.

**02:18–02:30 | Close.** Mention the app runs locally with no login, and explicitly state that it is a first-pass heuristic rather than a replacement for human or assistive-technology testing.
